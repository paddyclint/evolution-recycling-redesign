Who We Are

One of Ireland's Leading Scrap Metal Processors.

Founded in 1994, Evolution Recycling moved to a fully purpose-built, concreted site in 2011 to keep pace with demand. Today we process thousands of tonnes of ferrous and non-ferrous metal every year — sorted, processed, weighed and prepared for domestic and international markets.
