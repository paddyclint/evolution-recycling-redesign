Skip Hire & Site Clearance.

Site Clearance · 29 June 2026 · 3 min read

Renovations, demolitions, and unit clean-outs all produce the same problem: a growing pile of metal that's in the way of the actual job. Here's how to plan the scrap side of it so it doesn't become its own project.

Decide between a skip and a collection run

If scrap is going to build up steadily over days or weeks — a renovation, a strip-out, an ongoing fit-out — a lockable skip on site is usually easier than repeated van runs. We deliver and collect lockable skips throughout Ireland, and the lock matters: an open skip of copper or cable outside a site is a standing invitation for someone else to help themselves to it overnight.

For a one-off clearance where everything comes out at once — a full strip-out, a vehicle, a unit being emptied — a single collection visit is usually simpler. Either way, a quick call beforehand means we arrive with the right size vehicle or skip for the job.

Sort as you go, if you can

You don't have to be precise, but separating steel from copper, cable, and motors as the job progresses — even just into rough piles — means a faster, more accurate quote on collection day, rather than one blended price for an unsorted mountain. See our guide on telling ferrous and non-ferrous metal apart if you want a head start.

What it doesn't cover

One thing worth knowing upfront: we don't take cars as part of scrap collection or skip hire — vehicle disposal needs to go through an authorised treatment facility. Everything else — structural steel, appliances, cabling, motors, fixtures — we'll take.

On the day

Whatever arrives gets weighed and paid on the spot, with cash settled within 24 hours if it needs more time to grade. No waiting around for a callback with a number.
