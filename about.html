30+ Years in Scrap Metal.

Our Story

Built on the Yard Floor, Not a Boardroom.

Evolution Recycling was founded in 1994 and has grown into one of the leading collectors, processors and suppliers of scrap metal in Ireland. In 2011, we moved to a fully purpose-built, concreted site in Bluebell, Dublin 12 — built specifically to handle the growth in volume and the range of materials coming through our gates.

Three decades in, the principle hasn't changed: weigh it accurately, pay a fair price, and get it back into the supply chain properly.

What Happens On Site

Thousands of Tonnes, Sorted Properly.

Every year our Dublin 12 facility processes thousands of tonnes of both ferrous and non-ferrous metal — copper, aluminium, brass, stainless steel, lead and steel. Materials are sorted, processed, weighed and prepared for both domestic and international markets.

It's hands-on work, done by people who know the difference between a load that's ready to move and one that needs more separation first.
